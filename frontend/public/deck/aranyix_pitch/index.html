Act VII · Carbon

Carbon quantification done honestly

A range, not a marketing number — engine byot-carbon-1.3.0

  • Three methodologies: IPCC AR6, Verra VM0047, Gold Standard LUF
  • Species-specific allometrics plus Chave 2014 pan-tropical model
  • Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation — 90% confidence interval on every CO₂e figure
  • Verra-style conservative deduction when uncertainty exceeds 15%
  • Mortality-adjusted ex-ante lifetime credits, not naive multiplication
  • Dynamic permanence buffer 10–30% driven by NPRT risk assessment
  • Additional pools: deadwood, litter and soil organic carbon
