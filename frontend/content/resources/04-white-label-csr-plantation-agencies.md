# White-Label CSR Plantation for Event Agencies in India

Your team already wins the room on event day. Brands still ask later: **what happened to the trees?**

That question arrives in Q3 when the sustainability head reviews Schedule VII spend, or when procurement compares your proposal to a competitor who promises “500 trees planted” at half your fee. The agency that answers with **geo-tagged proof, survival tracking, and scheduled client reports** keeps the retainer. The agency that stops at photos competes on price forever.

This guide explains how experiential and CSR agencies can white-label a **Plant → Track → Report** layer, productise multi-year care plans, and sell serious ESG fulfilment without becoming a software company.

## The agency offer that closes

Position a **CSR Event Pack** as fulfilment infrastructure:

- Planting operations (your crew, partner NGO, or co-managed)  
- 1 / 2 / 3 year care plans with documented maintenance visits  
- Portal passports, scheduled PDF or dashboard reports, and export packs for client ESG teams  
- Optional satellite health screening on larger community blocks  

You keep the client relationship and creative narrative; the MRV partner runs evidence collection, QA rules, and reporting cadence under your brand guidelines.

White-label does not mean anonymous. It means the client sees **your agency** as the programme owner while a specialist platform handles geo-tags, survival workflows, and audit-prep exports.

## Packaging tiers agencies can quote

**Tier 1 — Event capture**  
Single-day registration, site map, initial photo pack, 30-day QA closeout. Best for pilot clients or smaller campuses.

**Tier 2 — Seasonal care**  
Tier 1 plus 3/6/12 month survival checks and two scheduled client reports. Best for annual CSR retainers.

**Tier 3 — Programme partnership**  
Multi-site registry, executive dashboard access for client ESG team, quarterly exports, optional BRSR-aligned tables. Best for national accounts.

Price care plans as recurring programme lines, not as a hidden cost in planting day. Maintenance is where margins and impact both live.

## Delivery workflow (simple RACI)

- **Agency:** client contract, creative, volunteer experience, programme branding  
- **NGO / nursery partner:** planting quality, local permissions, on-ground crew  
- **MRV platform:** mobile app, geo-tag QA, survival scheduling, exports  
- **Client ESG contact:** species approval, report format sign-off, assurance handoff  

Document this RACI in your proposal so procurement sees operational maturity.

## Client reporting without over-claiming

Train client-facing teams to use honest language:

- “Geo-tagged plantation registry with survival monitoring” — yes  
- “Certified carbon removal” — no, unless client completes a separate registry process  
- “Audit-ready evidence pack” — yes, with caveat that assurance is external  
- “Guaranteed 100% survival” — never  

Aranyix provides MRV and audit-prep tooling. It does not issue Verra, Gold Standard, or other registry credits, and it is not a certification body. Agencies protect client trust by mirroring that honesty in decks and SOWs.

## FAQ

**Do we white-label the mobile app?**  
Most programmes brand the reports and client portal first. Full app white-label is rarely required for CSR events; confirm with your MRV partner.

**Who owns the plantation data?**  
Contract should state client ownership with agency operator access during the programme term. Clarify export rights at contract end.

**Can we mix volunteer planting and professional maintenance?**  
Yes. Register volunteers as planters; assign maintenance crews separately in the same registry so survival checks stay consistent.

**What if the client only wants one event?**  
Sell Tier 1, but show the upgrade path to Tier 2. Clients often return once they see the first report.

**How do we handle multi-city rollouts?**  
Use project scoping per city with shared species and QA rules. National dashboards impress enterprise clients.
