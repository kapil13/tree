Compensatory afforestation is planted because forest land was diverted. A monitoring visit, public audit, or National Green Tribunal question later asks **where the plantation is, and whether it survived.** Forest department and CAMPA teams, user agencies, and planting agencies or NGOs often cannot answer from one geo-tagged record.

## The buyer problem: the plantation exists on paper until someone asks for the point

Mining, infrastructure, and linear-project user agencies divert forest land under the Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 and carry a compensatory afforestation obligation. The Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016 provides for the monies collected from those agencies and for authorities — national, and in each State and Union territory — to administer the funds. State CAMPA authorities apply the state fund to plantations and related forestry works. After planting, the file is often a diversion note, a journal, phone photos, and a polygon nobody has walked.

Teams searching for **compensatory afforestation monitoring India** meet that gap when concurrent monitoring, audit, or a court or NGT question arrives. Staff rebuild **CAMPA geo-tagged plantation evidence** from PDFs, and survival after summer and monsoon is a narrative rather than a resurvey. The 2016 Act records the need for independent concurrent monitoring and evaluation. A field record helps show the work. It does not replace the statutory filing.

## Plant — register the CA block before the planting day becomes a memory

**Plant** is registration of the compensatory site, not the season-end count.

1. Map the CA polygon or block to the diverted-forest reference and the land identified for afforestation, on or before planting day.  
2. Capture GPS or a polygon, species, photos, density notes, planting year, and the programme tag — compensatory afforestation, or assisted natural regeneration only where that was approved.  
3. Assign a digital ID so resurveys attach to the same block.  
4. Link the user agency, implementing agency or NGO, nursery, and work order to that diversion and CA site code.

Offline Android capture covers range and NGO crews without signal; the web view is for division and state CAMPA review. Without clean Plant data, Track and Report name a site no one can stand on. Keep a nearby CSR plantation day on its own tag. See [geo-tagged tree plantation for CSR](/resources/geo-tagged-tree-plantation-csr-india).

## Track — CAMPA plantation survival tracking on a cadence reviewers expect

**Track** is a repeat visit with a status. **CAMPA plantation survival tracking** is that visit, tied back to the same block.

| Interval | Typical purpose |
|---|---|
| 0–30 days | Fix mis-tags and boundary gaps; freeze the baseline CA registry |
| 3 months | First mortality signal; replacement while the season is still open |
| 6 months | Summer or monsoon stress check on the block |
| 12 months | Annual monitoring, audit, and scheme-review anchor |

Crews record alive / stressed / dead / replaced, with a photo when status changes. Managers roll survival by CA site, division, user agency, implementing agency, and year. Optional NDVI and SAR layers only choose which blocks to walk. They do not replace ground truth for an audit or NGT sample. Put resurvey ownership in the work order. The same loop is in [plantation MRV software India](/resources/plantation-mrv-software-india).

## Report — what the export pack contains

**Report** is the PDF / XLSX pack a monitoring meeting, an audit sample, or a court or NGT file can open without a rebuild. The export pack contains:

- CA site or tree IDs, GPS or polygon, species, planting year, and the diversion or CA land reference  
- User agency and implementing agency or NGO on the same row  
- Survival snapshot for the window, including replacements  
- Photo references tied to the visit, not an undated album  
- Methodology notes: GPS accuracy, how mortality and replacement are defined, and who resurveyed  
- Limitations: blocks not yet resurveyed, and modeled CO₂e labeled as an estimate only

That pack is **compensatory afforestation MRV**. Carry it into statutory portals and filings, including geo-tagged records on e-Green Watch. ## FAQ

**What should CAMPA afforestation monitoring actually record?**  
A geo-tagged CA block or tree, the diversion or CA land it answers to, species, planting year, survival resurveys, and an export a monitoring or audit team can sample.

**Who uses the same record?**  
State CAMPA teams sample survival and location. User agencies show the obligation can be found. Implementing agencies and NGOs keep a workflow that still makes sense at the next resurvey.

**Is digital MRV a CAMPA approval or a Green Credit?**  
No. MRV is measurement and monitoring evidence. Approvals, fund-use certification, and Green Credit issuance stay with the competent authority. Aranyix does not issue Green Credits or registry credits.

**Can Aranyix replace e-Green Watch or other statutory CAMPA portals?**  
No. It is complementary programme MRV and audit-prep evidence. Portal uploads and statutory returns stay with the department or the user agency.

**How does this relate to mine green belts and CSR plantations?**  
A mine that diverted forest land may hold both an environmental-clearance green belt and a compensatory afforestation obligation. Keep the CA register separate from CSR event counts. See [mine green belt plantation monitoring](/resources/mine-green-belt-plantation-monitoring-india) and [geo-tagged tree plantation for CSR](/resources/geo-tagged-tree-plantation-csr-india).

**How big should a first pilot be?**  
One CA block, one division, or one linear stretch. Prove Plant → Track → Report in 90 days, then extend across the planting year.
